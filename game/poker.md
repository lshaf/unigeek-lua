# Video Poker

A 5-credit bet is taken each deal. Hold the cards you want, draw new ones for the rest, and you're paid for the resulting hand. Payouts (per bet): Royal Flush 250x, Straight Flush 50x, Four 25x, Full House 9x, Flush 6x, Straight 4x, Three 3x, Two Pair 2x, a pair of Jacks-or-better 1x. Your bankroll persists; if you bust, a fresh 100-credit stake is granted.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| UP / DOWN | move the selector (5 cards, then the DEAL/DRAW button) |
| OK | toggle HOLD on a card / press DRAW / press DEAL for a new hand |
| BACK | exit (bankroll is saved) |

Bankroll persists to /unigeek/games/poker.txt.
