# Ohm's Law

Pick which two of voltage (V), current (I), resistance (R) and power (P) you know; the other two are calculated from Ohm's law and P = V*I. Values are entered as text so decimals work (e.g. 3.3, 0.02). Units are volts / amps / ohms / watts — convert mA to A yourself (20 mA = 0.02).

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| OK | run another calculation |
| BACK | exit |

In-memory only.
